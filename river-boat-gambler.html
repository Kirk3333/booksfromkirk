River Boat Gambler

A novel based on the true life of George Devol.

George Devol was the most notorious card sharp ever to work the Mississippi. He cheated thousands out of fortunes, got caught more times than he could count, and lived to tell every story with a grin.

It begins with a ten-year-old cabin boy frozen in a doorway, watching four men at a card table and finding the only church he would ever truly attend.
