Write a Review

Not sure what to say?

You don't need to write an essay. Most helpful reviews are two or three sentences. If you're stuck, any one of these is plenty:

  • What kept you turning pages?
  • Which character stayed with you?
  • Who would you hand this book to?
  • Did it feel real?

Honest is the only thing I ask for. A thoughtful three-star review helps the right readers find the book just as much as a five-star one does.
