The Queen of Ohio

How one woman swindled the richest men in America.

Cassie Chadwick convinced the wealthiest men in the country that she was Andrew Carnegie's secret daughter. They lined up to hand her their fortunes — and thanked her for the privilege.

A novel based on the true life of one of the most audacious con artists in American history, told in her own unrepentant voice. You're going to like her. Everyone did, right up until they couldn't afford to.
