The ride begins in Durango.

Welcome to the Canyon Outlaws Motorcycle Club, where loyalty, strategy, and unbreakable bonds define every mile. When Jerry, the club's sharp-minded treasurer, meets Riley — a resourceful, brave waitress in a restaurant on a lonely back road — their instant connection sparks something deeper than the road.

As lovers and true partners, they navigate high-stakes challenges: forging alliances, outsmarting rivals, and expanding the club's territory across the Southwest. Amid the thunder of engines, they steal quiet getaways under starlit skies — moments of tenderness that fuel their love and the club's future.

Will their bond secure the club's growth… or become its greatest strength?
